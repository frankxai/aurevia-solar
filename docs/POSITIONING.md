## One line

**German solar engineering, distilled into installable intelligence.**

## What Aurevia is

Aurevia packages the engineering discipline behind a real German solar-carport system —
100×100 mm structural aluminum, bifacial glass-glass canopies, built and shipped from a
real warehouse in Seesen — into digital tools any AI assistant can run: skills, agent
workflows, prompt packs, calculation engines, and a white-label platform template.

The hardware is the provenance. The software is the product. The proof page is the marketing.

## Who it is for (ICP)

1. **The installer-operator** (DACH + EU): runs a solar or carport business, uses ChatGPT or
   Claude daily, wants quotes, cutting lists, and visuals faster — without hiring. Buys the
   BOM engine and the swarm. Feels: *finally, tools built by someone who has stood under a
   carport, not just prompted about one.*
2. **The architect / planner** (global): needs photorealistic solar-integrated renders and
   defensible pre-dimensioning for client conversations. Buys the prompt pack and the free
   skill. Feels: *this respects my craft — real camera values, real material names, real
   module dimensions.*
3. **The AI-forward builder** (global): engineers, agency owners, indie hackers who sell to
   the solar industry. Buys the white-label template and the swarm. Feels: *this is the rare
   AI product that shows its receipts.*

## The feeling we engineer

Walking into a German master workshop: warm paper, precise tools on hairline racks, one
object in the light. Not a casino, not a SaaS gradient. The visitor should think:

> "These people measure. If they say a skill can size a canopy, it can — and they show me
> the transcript where it did."

Captivation comes from **witnessed capability**, not adjectives: the exploded CAD view they
can scrub, the eval transcript they can read, the free skill they can install in 60 seconds
and test against their own roof.

## The transformation we sell

**From prompt-guessing to engineered output.**

Before: generic AI answers, invented numbers, renders with melted module grids, quotes that
embarrass you in front of a client.
After: assumptions stated, geometry that respects physics, visuals with correct materials,
documents you can hand to a Fachbetrieb — with the responsibility line already drawn.

## Why we win (and the moat)

- **Provenance:** every constant traces to real hardware a real family business builds.
  Competitors prompt about solar; we measured it.
- **Radical proof:** we publish what each tool produced in tests — including what it cannot
  do. In a market of inflated AI claims, honesty is the differentiation.
- **Clean responsibility:** outputs are planning aids ("Planungsgrundlage"); structural,
  electrical, and permit authority stays with professionals. Buyers get power without
  liability confusion; we sell with none.

## Voice rules

- Calm, exact, workshop-grade. Short declaratives. Numbers only when generated or counted.
- Say "in development" plainly. Say "we withdrew v0.1 because its math was wrong" plainly —
  the correction note is a trust asset, not a weakness.
- Banned: superlatives ("world's first"), urgency theater, "AI-powered" filler,
  "revolutionary", any claim a structural engineer would wince at.
- English is the default register: plain, international, no Denglish marketing mash. German
  terms appear where they are the precise term (Planungsgrundlage, Fachbetrieb) with a
  gloss on first use.

## Page-level copy direction

- **Home:** Thesis (one line) → the proof object (exploded CAD, scrubbable) → three products
  max → the responsibility line → route hardware interest to SolarCarport.tech / PV Lager.
  Hero ≤ 10 words; subhead ≤ 20.
- **/forge (catalog):** each card answers: what it is, what exists today, what it costs (or
  planned price, clearly marked), which license, link to its proof entry. No badges that
  imply sales history.
- **/proof:** per product: assistants tested, verbatim inputs and outputs, boundary list
  ("what it refused / got wrong"), date, version. This page is allowed to be unflattering —
  that is its job.
- **Product detail:** deliverable manifest (generated), sample preview, license text,
  responsibility line, waitlist or download. Nothing else.

## Naming
